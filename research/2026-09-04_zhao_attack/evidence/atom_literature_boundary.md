# Literature boundary for the low-multiplicity tail

Read on 2026-09-04: O. Ordaz, A. Philipp, I. Santos, W. A. Schmid, *On the Olson and the Strong Davenport constants*, Journal de Theorie des Nombres de Bordeaux 23 (2011), 715--750.

Primary full text: https://jtnb.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/jtnb.784.pdf

Section 8, printed pp.746--748, describes a finite computation in C_5^4 using 26 normalized five-element starting sets. It reports SD(C_5^4)=16 and O(C_5^4)=17. In particular, low multiplicity does not rule out long zero-sum-free sequences: the Olson value yields a squarefree zero-sum-free sequence of length 16.

The same section reports a total of 52869 CPU hours for its 26 test runs, measured against an AMD Opteron 8356 (2.3GHz) core. This is a methodological boundary, not an estimate of our own software's required running time. It warns against treating the remaining general squarefree or low-multiplicity case as an automatically small enumeration.

This paper is not used to establish any exclusion of an endpoint counterexample in atom_structure.md. Its values do not imply either target A or B: they concern arbitrary nonempty zero sums or squarefree minimal atoms, whereas A/B impose a shorter cutoff on longer, possibly repeated sequences.
